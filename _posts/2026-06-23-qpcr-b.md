# Relative Quantification Using the Delta-Delta Ct Method

## Introduction

Quantitative PCR (qPCR) is commonly used to measure relative changes in gene expression between experimental groups. In this exercise, gene expression was compared between a DMSO control group and an inhibitor treatment group.

Tubulin was used as the reference gene because its expression remained stable across treatments. The expression of all target genes was normalized to Tubulin before comparing the treatment and control groups.

---

## Step 1: Calculate ΔCt

The first step is to normalize the expression of each target gene to the reference gene.

ΔCt = Ct(Target Gene) − Ct(Reference Gene)

For example, for the gene **ascs**:

### DMSO Control

Ct(ascs) = 29.094

Ct(Tubulin) = 23.296

ΔCt = 29.094 − 23.296

ΔCt = 5.798

### Inhibitor Treatment

Ct(ascs) = 28.509

Ct(Tubulin) = 23.296

ΔCt = 28.509 − 23.296

ΔCt = 5.213

This normalization removes variation caused by differences in RNA quantity or cDNA synthesis efficiency.

---

## Step 2: Calculate ΔΔCt

The control group is used as the baseline.

ΔΔCt = ΔCt(Treatment) − ΔCt(Control)

For the gene **ascs**:

ΔΔCt = 5.213 − 5.798

ΔΔCt = -0.585

---

## Step 3: Calculate Fold Change

Relative expression is calculated using:

Fold Change = 2^(-ΔΔCt)

For the gene **ascs**:

Fold Change = 2^(0.585)

Fold Change = 1.50

This indicates that expression of **ascs** increased approximately 1.5-fold in the inhibitor treatment group compared with the control.

## Interpretation of Fold Change Values

A fold change greater than 1 indicates upregulation of the gene.

A fold change less than 1 indicates downregulation of the gene.

The strongest increase was observed for **NGN**, which showed approximately a two-fold increase in expression following inhibitor treatment.

The strongest decrease was observed for **pitx**, whose expression decreased to approximately 24% of the control level.

Genes such as **soxC**, **foxA**, and **ascs** also showed increased expression after treatment.

In contrast, **pitx**, **SM30**, **sm50**, and **opt** showed reduced expression in the inhibitor-treated samples.
